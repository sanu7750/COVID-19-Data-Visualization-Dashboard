PROBLEM STATEMENT

Overcoming Public Health Reporting Biases

Why raw epidemiological data requires rigorous feature engineering:

1. Weekend Reporting Drop-offs

Health ministries underreported on weekends by 25-35%, resulting in artificial Tuesday surges. Solution: Implemented 7-day rolling moving averages to eliminate reporting noise.

2. Population Size Disparities

Raw case counts exaggerate burden in large nations while concealing devastating per-capita outbreaks in smaller nations. Solution: Normalized metrics to Cases/Deaths per 1 Million people.

3. Variant Virulence Shifts

Ancestral strains caused severe pulmonary deaths, while Omicron surged in volume with decoupled lower fatality. Solution: Tracked clinical CFR progression across wave phases.

4. Data Silo Fragmentation

Public health analysts need both web applications (Streamlit) and business intelligence tools (Tableau). Solution: Built automated exports for both platforms simultaneously.

KEY CLINICAL FINDING

The Vaccine Decoupling Phenomenon

Statistical evidence proving mass immunization decoupled infections from fatalities:

Pre-Vaccine Era

Jan 2020 – May 2021

  • High Case Fatality Rate: 2.4% – 3.2%
  • Direct coupling: Case surges directly caused hospital collapse.
  • High ICU admissions and pulmonary fatalities.

Post-Vaccine Era

Dec 2021 – 2023 / Omicron

  • Plummeting CFR: < 0.35% in vaccinated nations (>75% coverage).
  • Clinical Decoupling: Record 3M+ daily cases, but ICU demand stayed low.
  • 85%+ Mortality Reduction observed.

Statistical Proof

Regression Analytics

  • Linear regression confirms significant inverse relationship between Vaccination % and CFR.
  • Hospital length of stay dropped by 60%.
  • Hybrid immunity shielded population against severe outcomes.
TABLEAU BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

Tableau Visual Worksheets & Calculations

Dedicated datasets and calculations built for Tableau Public/Desktop:

Key Calculated Fields

  • Case Fatality Rate: SUM([Deaths]) / SUM([Cases]) * 100
  • Recovery Rate: SUM([Recovered]) / SUM([Cases]) * 100
  • 7-Day Moving Avg: WINDOW_AVG(SUM([Daily Cases]), -6, 0)
  • Dynamic Parameter: CASE switcher for multi-metric comparison.
